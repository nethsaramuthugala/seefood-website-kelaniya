    About Us

    At A.K.J Seafood Kitchen & Restaurant we bring the catch of the day straight to the tava. Tuna, sailfish, prawns and cuttlefish — chopped, tossed and served hot with the bold flavours Sri Lanka loves.

    Whether it's a quick half kottu after work or a family platter that feeds five, every plate is made fresh when you order. Wash it down with a fresh woodapple juice or a smooth cappuccino.

    • Fresh seafood daily
    • Platters for 5 people
    • Kottu with roti, noodles, pasta or string hoppers
    • Dine-in & takeaway
